Calculation Steps

1. Saturation Pressures (Antoine)

Psat = 10^(A - B/(C + T))

2. Relative Humidity

RH = (Pw / Psat_db) × 100

3. Absolute Humidity

AH = (Pw × M_H₂O) / (R × T)

4. Specific Humidity

ω = 0.622 × (Pw / (P - Pw))

Calculation Steps

1. Saturation Pressure (Antoine)

log₁₀(Psat) = A - (B / (C + T))

2. Vapor Pressure

Pw = (RH / 100) × Psat

3. Mole Fractions (Dalton's Law)

y_H₂O = Pw / P

y_N₂ = 0.79 × (1 - y_H₂O)

4. Mass Fractions

mf = (y × M) / M_mixture
